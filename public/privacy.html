Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Same Place? records your answers anonymously so people's choices can be compared with the address-matching models in the game. The results may be written up in a follow-up study. No account, name or contact details are ever collected.

Recording my answers: …

Your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, so nothing is recorded regardless of this setting.

What is recorded

One entry for each pair you answer:

  • which address pair it was, what you answered, and whether that was right;
  • how long you took to answer, and whether the tab was in the background meanwhile;
  • its position in the round, and the opponent and difficulty you picked;
  • whether the screen is phone-sized or larger, and the game version;
  • a random code for the current visit and one for the current round, so answers from the same round or visit can be grouped. The visit code is forgotten when you close the tab, so separate visits can't be linked to each other.

What is not recorded

  • No name, email, account or login.
  • No IP address or location is stored with your answers.
  • No typing or free text, no cookies, no advertising or cross-site tracking.

Why

To learn which address pairs people find hard, how long they take, and whether people and models get the same pairs wrong. Results will be shared only as summaries, or as an anonymous dataset of the fields listed above.

Where it is stored and for how long

Answers are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. The game itself is served by Vercel, and its fonts by Google Fonts. Like most web services, these providers may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability; those logs are not part of the study data. Answers are kept for up to three years, after which they are deleted or published only as the anonymous dataset described above.

Your choices

  • Turn recording off with the switch above. It applies to this browser on this device.
  • If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, nothing is recorded.
  • Because answers aren't linked to you, there's no way to look up or delete a particular person's answers afterwards. Turning recording off stops new answers from being saved.
